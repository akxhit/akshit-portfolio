Redesigning a Car Auction App Experience

This case study highlights how I redesigned a car auction platform to improve trust and transparency for dealers.

Context

I was part of 10K Designers (a product design cohort). This is my capstone project in collaboration with Cars24 to gain experience in a real-life project.

About Cars24

Partner App (B2B) : Provides car dealers with access to a verified inventory, bidding tools, and efficient procurement processes, alongside support services like financing and logistics.

My target users are Pan India registered car dealers.

How big this market is

The used car market in India was valued at approximately USD 26.85 billion in 2023.

Expected to reach USD 31.62 billion by 2024.

Projected to grow to USD 63.87 billion by 2029, with a CAGR of 15.10%.

Cars24 operates in 286 cities across India, indicating a vast network for vehicle auctions.

Vehicles listed in bank auctions can be available at prices 20% to 30% lower than the market value, providing significant savings opportunities for buyers.

Setting up the Context

Ever tried buying a car at an auction? Now imagine doing that daily as your job. Car dealers are stuck with outdated auction systems that waste time and limit options. Experienced dealers miss opportunities, while newcomers feel overwhelmed. They all want a better way to access more cars, bid from anywhere, and enjoy the process.

Problem

Current car auction apps in India lack transparency and energy, leaving dealers isolated and uncertain. They struggle with inaccurate information, hidden damages, and a cumbersome bidding process, making it hard to build trust, especially for new users.

User Problems

Difficulty analyzing the inspection report, which reduces transparency.

Lack of educational guidance for first-time bidders.

Uncertainty regarding whether price increases are driven by users or the platform itself.

Platform is very boring and doesn't give the thrill you get in real life auctions.

Business Problems

Decreased Auction Success Rates: Auctions are often underbid due to unclear processes and lack of confidence in vehicle information, reducing overall auction value and business revenue.

User Retention Challenges: New dealers especially feel overwhelmed and drop off early in the process, impacting user retention and long-term engagement.

Competitive Benchmarking

To create a meaningful solution, I knew I had to understand what’s already working—and where the gaps are. Since these auction apps aren’t publicly accessible, I took a different approach. I spent time on competitor websites, watched YouTube and app reviews, and studied screenshots to see how users were interacting with these platforms.

Cars24 focuses on scale, Spinny on a curated experience, but the human element and enthusiasm are missing. Dealers need trust, transparency, and simplicity, yet these are often lacking.


With that in mind, I aimed to blend the best features while addressing key pain points, creating an auction experience that dealers could genuinely trust and feel excited about.

Home screen

Unlike competitors, I introduced a dedicated home screen in the app to provide users with a centralized hub for their auction activities. Competitor platforms often rely on fragmented flows, but I aimed for a streamlined, all-in-one experience right from the start and aimed to provide a better onboarding experience by clarifying their doubts in the first fold itself.

User Impact

Quick Info Access: Users can view auctions, bids, and statuses instantly, reducing navigation time and effort.

Personalization: The home screen updates based on user preferences for a tailored experience.

Confidence Boost: Critical details up front help users, especially newbies, feel more confident in navigating the app.

Business Impact

Increased Engagement: Centralized key actions lead to more frequent usage and quicker decision-making.

Retention & Trust: Clear activity tracking improves transparency, boosting user retention.

Better Dealer Performance: Easily accessible auction insights lead to faster bids and higher completion rates, driving revenue.

Overall Impact on Users & Business

User Flexibility: Allowing users to switch between views gives them control over their browsing experience, improving satisfaction and usability.

Increased User Retention: Whether it’s power users needing quick access to multiple listings or newbies needing more detailed views, the dual-view approach ensures everyone’s needs are met, leading to increased retention.

Enhanced Auction Participation: With easy access to both summary and detailed views, users are more likely to participate in multiple auctions, increasing bidding activity and driving up final sale prices.
